Adversarial Threat Model

Why standard RAG fails on evidence

01 ELA Threshold 25.0

Forged or Spliced Scans

Standard OCR reads manipulated numbers without checking pixel integrity. JPEG Error Level Analysis (32x32 block ELA, threshold 25.0) catches altered totals such as $18,000 spliced onto a $12,000 receipt.

02 Pre-LLM Sanitization

Hidden PDF Injections

White-on-white text (rgb(255,255,255)) and instruction overrides hijack downstream LLM outputs. PyMuPDF strips hidden spans and off-page override text before the LLM ever sees it.

03 Temporal Graph

Temporal Amendments

Legal addenda updating amounts over time look like conflicting facts to flat chunk retrieval. SUPERSEDES edges separate valid legal updates ($4,500 to $5,200) from true contradictions.

04 Abstention Gate

Illegible and Blank Fields

Vector RAG hallucinates answers when scans are blurry or ledger cells are blank. Laplacian blur checks (< 80.0) and [MISSING] table flags trigger deterministic abstention instead of hallucination.

Deterministic Flow

Four stages, one hard gate

01

Pre-LLM Forensics & Ingestion

Computes SHA-256 document IDs and runs JPEG ELA, Laplacian blur checks, hidden PDF span filtering, EXIF inspection, and prompt-injection stripping before text enters any prompt.

02

Atomic Claims & Entity Resolution

Rejects unsanitized chunks. Extracts Pydantic v2 provenance-bound claims via schema-constrained qwen2.5:3b or deterministic fallback, resolving entities with RapidFuzz and BAAI/bge-m3.

Pydantic v2 qwen2.5:3b RapidFuzz bge-m3
03

Evidence Graph & Trust Propagation

Builds a NetworkX MultiDiGraph linking Source, Claim, and Entity nodes with ASSERTS, ABOUT, SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, and SUPERSEDES edges across 5 iterative trust propagation passes.

NetworkX MultiDiGraph 5 Passes
04

Bounded BFS & Abstention Gate

Runs 2-hop bounded BFS retrieval plus local ChromaDB hints, then enforces Python's hard 0.65 abstention gate across VERIFIED, CONFLICT_RESOLVED, TAMPER_DETECTED, and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
